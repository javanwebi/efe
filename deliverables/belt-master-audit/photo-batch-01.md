# Industrial belt photo batch 01

Review date: 2026-11-19.

The 96 historical files were reviewed in contact sheets and selected markings
were inspected at larger size. The batch activates 28 existing family photographs
and 10 existing exact SKU photographs. The other 58 lack sufficient evidence on
the photographed product, brand/profile/size, or uniqueness of the view. The
historical supplier availability check remains a record of the previous review.

The first 50 eligible engineered product families were ranked by descending SKU
count, excluding families with newly activated historical photographs. They contain 4,450
snapshot SKUs. Every SKU was screened against the Allaoui public product sitemap
(20,212 URLs) for a listing URL containing its brand and exact profile/size.
That index yielded zero matching candidates. Targeted public-source searches
also covered selected families and exact models, including SWR, Dongil, Marshal,
Forza and Megadyne. The index screen does not cover other distributors and
individual open-web searches for the remaining SKUs are incomplete.

Nine family photographs and two exact SKU photographs were freshly downloaded
and encoded as WebP. Six of the 50 families gained a gallery photograph, and one
additional family outside the top 50 gained a gallery photograph. Two
SKUs in the batch gained exact photographs: 714 (Dongil Super Star B-50) and
1075 (Dongil Super Star REC BX-85). The batch remains open because the target of
three to four distinct photographs per family has not been reached.

The generated storefront manifest now includes 37 family photographs and 12
exact SKU photographs. Photos are approved on physical appearance and technical
identity. Approval records retain a source, review date, evidence and SHA-256.
